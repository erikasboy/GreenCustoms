## What We Do

<!-- TODO: owner to update -->

The shop handles the full range of guitar repair work — from quick setups to more involved restoration projects. If your guitar isn't playing the way it should, there's usually a straightforward fix.

- Action setup and intonation
- Fret level, crown, and polish
- Partial or full refrets
- Nut replacement and shaping
- Electronics repair — pots, switches, jacks, output issues
- Full wiring overhauls
- Crack repair and structural work
- Tuner replacement
- Bridge and saddle work (acoustic and electric)
- General cleaning and conditioning

## What to Expect

<!-- TODO: owner to update turnaround times -->

Drop-off or ship-in work is accepted. Every instrument gets a bench evaluation before any work starts — you'll hear back with a diagnosis and quote before anything is touched.

Turnaround time depends on the job and current backlog. Simple setups can often be done same-week. More involved work is discussed when you get in touch. Nothing leaves the shop until it plays right.
